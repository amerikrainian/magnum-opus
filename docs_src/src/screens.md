# Screens

## Title screen

"Press any key to continue": any key goes on to the campaign map.

## Campaign map

Three groups (Tab moves between them):

1. **The chapter's items**: puzzles, cutscenes, letters and solitaire, each with its kind and
   whether it is completed. A locked item reads "locked". Enter opens the item.
2. **Chapters**: every chapter of the campaign, the current one selected, locked ones marked.
   Enter switches chapter.
3. **Journal, the campaign switch, Options.** The campaign switch moves between Opus Magnum and
   De Re Metallica (or opens its store page when you do not own it).

When you come back to the map after finishing something, the game plays its unlock sequence; the
mod speaks each step ("Lesson: Introduction unlocked").

Escape opens the pause menu, as in the game.

## Letters

Each part of the letter is a line; arrow through them. Close (or Escape) puts the letter away.

## Cutscenes

Each line is spoken as it appears, with the speaker's name. Enter, Space or Tab goes to the next
line; Escape skips a cutscene you have seen before.

## Puzzle info

Before a puzzle opens in the editor:

1. **Solutions**: your saved solutions with their scores. Moving onto one selects it; Enter opens
   it.
2. **New, Open, Rename, Copy, Delete.** Rename and Delete ask in a dialog.
3. **Leaderboards**: why there are none to show yet, when that is the case.
4. **Story**: the puzzle's story so far.

## Dialogs

A dialog reads its message and its buttons. In a text field (renaming a solution) type the new
name; each character is echoed. Enter confirms, Escape cancels.

## Pause menu, Options and Controls

The pause menu offers Options, Controls, Exit Game and Close. Options lists every setting:
dropdowns, on and off settings (Enter switches them), the two volumes (Left and Right Arrow, in
steps of ten percent), and the buttons. Its second group is the story panel. Controls lists the
game's controls.

## Sigmar's Garden

The solitaire. The game deals the first board once the first part of the conversation beside it
is read.

1. **Story**: the conversation so far, a line at a time. Enter on any line (or Space anywhere)
   shows the next one. Where the next part waits for wins, the last line says so ("Win 1 game to
   continue"). When no game is in play the screen opens here; otherwise it opens on the board.
2. **Board**: a hex cursor, moved with Q (northwest), E (northeast), A (west), D (east), Z
   (southwest) and C (southeast), or the numpad's 7, 9, 4, 6, 1 and 3. Alt+Shift with them skips
   to the next hex that reads differently; Ctrl+Home returns to the centre, 0, 0. A hex reads its
   coordinates, then its marble, with "locked" where the marble is not free yet, and "selected"
   first when you have picked it. Enter picks the marble under the cursor: "selected", then
   "cleared" with both names when the second one matches. Comma and Period go to the previous
   and next free marble; X reads the neighbours.
3. **Marbles**: how many of each are left, as the strip under the board shows them, with
   "warning" where the game marks a count that cannot all be paired.
4. **New Game, Rules, Wins, Close.**

## Not yet covered

The solution editor (the puzzles themselves), the journal, the puzzle editor and the solution
recorder are not covered by this version. The mod names a screen it does not
know yet when it opens.
